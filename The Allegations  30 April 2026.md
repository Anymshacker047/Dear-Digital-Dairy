🔰 Case 2 – Financial Email / Defamation Claim

---

🧾 Case Summary

A male complainant reported that a finance company was repeatedly sending emails to his employer.
He claimed that these emails were defaming him professionally and putting his job at risk.

The situation became sensitive when the complainant expressed extreme distress, stating that he might harm himself if he lost his job.

---

🎯 Objective

To verify whether:

- The company was legitimate
- The communication was harassment
- Or a legally valid financial recovery process

---

🔍 Initial Evidence

- Two Gmail IDs
- One Phone Number
- Company Email (Google Workspace domain)

---

🧠 Investigation Process

1. Email & Domain Analysis

- The email was sent using a Google Workspace business domain
- No direct signs of spoofing or fake identity were observed

📌 Initial impression: Could be a legitimate business communication

---

2. Company OSINT

- Official website of the company was identified
- Hosting traced to
- Server IP address was obtained

---

3. Address Verification

- Office address was reviewed
- Using street-level observation, a GSTIN number was identified

📌 However, no strong actionable lead was derived at this stage

---

4. Identity Correlation

- A company email ID was found to be genuine

- Phone number analysis revealed:
  
  - It belonged to a company colleague (personal number)

- Two Gmail IDs identified:
  
  - One appeared to be staff-level (attendee)
  - One appeared to be head-level authority

---

🧠 Investigator’s Thought Process

At one stage, after nearly 2 hours without a clear lead, the investigation reached a pause point.

- I mentally reconstructed the entire case step-by-step
- Revisited every clue, every connection, and every assumption
- Even small details—like profile images—were observed repeatedly to catch any missed pattern

When no new direction emerged, the approach shifted:

➡️ Instead of focusing only on the company, I changed perspective

- Started reviewing the victim’s background over the past few years
- Analyzed what the individual does, possible financial behavior, and context

📌 This shift in thinking helped in forming a more realistic hypothesis

---

🧠 Analytical Insight

Possible scenario identified:

- The complainant may have a pending loan
- The company could be attempting official contact via workplace communication

⚠️ However, no conclusive proof was available at this stage

---

📢 Escalation

Time: ~5:30 PM

The case was reported to the senior officer.

💡 Key Learning (Case 2 – Day 1)

- Not all suspicious communication is fraudulent
- Investigation requires re-evaluating assumptions
- Changing perspective can unlock new insights
- Emotional distress of the complainant must be handled carefully
